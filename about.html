Heya, World.

I'm Heya. I research, write, create, and get people together.

My path started as a musician and led me to Bandcamp, where I spent 3.5 years as an engineer working toward helping independent artists get paid better. That experience sparked a curiosity about the material and social realities behind digital life.

Currently, I am finishing my degree at New Media at Aalto University in Espoo, Finland. My MA thesis traces how more-than-human bodies are exhausted by the cloud and the feed, and explores resistance through participatory design workshops.

Outside of participatory art and research, I have a creative writing practice. I like to weave in multiple languages and explore the topics of body, home, and identity.
