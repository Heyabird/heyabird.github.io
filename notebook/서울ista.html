서울ista

2026

Mie olen kotoisin 서울ista.

Miekin kasvoin Washington, USAssa.

Koska kasvoin 할무니 kanssa, puhun joskus 충청도-murretta.

I hella 사랑 nuudeleita ja 떡볶이.

Mie olen taiteilija ja tutkija.

Nimeni on Heya 해나 Kwon.

Nykyään olen Torniossa.

Keräsin vadelmia ystäviäni Veran kanssa 토르니오ssa

ja muistin, kuinka 할무니ni keräsi karhunvatukoita

서울issa ja 워싱턴issa.

About This Poem

I made this poem during a Meänkieli (the regional Finnic language spoken in the Torne Valley) poetry workshop I partook in Tornio, Finland, near the Finland-Sweden border.


The workshop facilitator and poet Linnea Huhta encouraged us to use multiple languages and dialects, and that "there is no wrong in poetry."


This became an opportunity for me to use not only Korean but also 충청도 dialect of Korean, in addition to English (including West Coast / Californian slangs like "hella") and Finnish (including Meänkieli that I learned while spending time in Tornio).


Inspired by the workshop, I have been scribbling down multi-language poems that mirror my identity, which often feels like an odd collage of three different cultures.
